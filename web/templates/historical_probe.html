If you added a device to EMT after Home Assistant had already been recording it, its earlier usage is sitting inside your house total with no way to split it out. Pick the sensor that measures each device's energy (kWh) below, then Check sensors to confirm the history looks right — how far back it goes, its cadence and any gaps — before you attribute anything. Add more than one sensor to a device to stitch across a Home Assistant change (a replaced device, a renamed entity, an integration swap). Checking reads only; it writes nothing.

Reconstructed history (device attribution)
Attribute history on a device above fills its usage onto the house blocks it wasn't recorded on yet — both pre-EMT history and live periods from before you added the device — split into your half-hours by the house import shape, clipped so a device can never exceed the metered import. A backup is taken first, it runs in the background (pausable), and it's fully reversible — if you picked the wrong sensor, Remove the device's reconstructed history below and try again. It never overwrites live-recorded device data.
