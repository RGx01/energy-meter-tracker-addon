🌿 Enable Carbon Insights

Carbon insights require a postcode prefix to fetch grid carbon intensity data from the National Grid ESO API (🇬🇧 UK only).
EMT records the actual grid intensity at the time of every half-hour block — enabling genuine per-period carbon accounting rather than monthly estimates.

Once a postcode is saved, carbon intensity recording begins on the next block finalise (~30 minutes). Carbon insights will appear after your first full billing period with data.
