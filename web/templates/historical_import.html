API import
Fetches each half-hour's kWh with its exact billed cost and off-peak/peak label — dispatch-aware, IOG included, reaching your full history. Imported blocks are flagged and reversible (Delete Blocks → reconstructed history).
How it works & timing
If a slot comes back without its cost (the supplier can drop it under load), the import quietly re-fetches and prices it from the real figure. It can take a while: the API is rate-limited and gets stricter the harder it's pushed, so the import runs in paced, resumable steps and eases off when the hourly allowance runs low — pausing and resuming on its own. Other apps on the same account share that allowance (the supplier's own app, the Home Assistant integration, any other API tool), so it may slow or pause while one of those is busy. Just leave it running. Afterwards a background pricing check gives the smart-charge (IOG) half-hours their exact off-peak price — rate-limited the same way, self-paced; the pricing-health panel below shows its progress.
Confirm your sites
Your Octopus account shows more than one address across this history. Name each past address so its data is filed under the right site and DNO region before the import runs. Your current site keeps its name (it's this instance's identity).
Reprice from CSV — last resort, if a price still won't fix
Almost every slot is fixed automatically by the verify pass or the pricing-health retry above. Only if a handful still won't price: download the consumption CSV for the affected period from your account, add each half-hour's unit rate (p/kWh) or cost, and upload it here to set the price straight from your bill — it corrects the existing blocks in place.
Fill from CSV
Download the pre-filled template for each channel — every half-hour's Start/End is already in it for this window. Add your figures (a unit rate (p/kWh) is easiest — we compute the cost from it; or enter the cost per half-hour instead), then upload the filled file. Imported blocks are flagged and reversible any time.
