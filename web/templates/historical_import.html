This is a read-only diagnostic. It reads Home Assistant's long-term statistics for each device's sensor(s) and reports how far back they go, their cadence, any gaps, whether they're energy or power, and how timestamps behave across DST. It writes nothing and creates no blocks. Your devices are listed below, each pre-filled with its currently-assigned sensor. The suggestions are filtered to energy (kWh) sensors, but you can paste any entity if yours doesn't have the right class or unit. Add more than one sensor to a device to stitch history together across a Home Assistant change (a replaced device, a renamed entity, an integration swap).
