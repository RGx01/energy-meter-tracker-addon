Octopus builds a bill from the ex-VAT figures — summing the raw half-hourly amounts and rounding at the subtotal before adding VAT (background: Guy Lipman). Turn this on to see each period's bill summary the Octopus way — ex-VAT per rate, the standing charge, a VAT row and the inc-VAT total — alongside EMT's usual Total Bill on the Billing chart.

Try it and compare it to your own bill. Octopus's PDF summary page uses a mixture of rounding — it rounds each band's kWh to 1 decimal — and its stated totals do not reflect its own half-hourly pricing in the detail pages at the back of the bill. The underlying logic and methodology aren't discernible from the document. EMT sums the real per-slot figures, so it reconciles to that half-hourly detail to within about a penny.

Bill-style rounding on the bill summary
Adds an ex-VAT bill-style breakdown to each period's bill summary — per rate, standing charge, a VAT row and the inc-VAT total. Your stored data and the existing Total Bill are unchanged — this is display only. It reads the ex-VAT figures EMT has captured, with a one-time backfill filling older history from your tariff; any slot still without a captured figure falls back to the period's VAT rate (read from your tariff, not a hardcoded 5%). It mirrors Octopus's presentation, not a guaranteed penny match — though against a real bill's half-hourly detail it's typically within a penny. Requires an API connection — a cost-sensor-only setup has no ex-VAT source, so the view stays hidden there rather than showing a bare assumption.
When on, each day's data table also shows the import rate and cost ex-VAT alongside the inc-VAT cost (export is zero-rated, so it's unchanged). These read the ex-VAT figures EMT captures — and a one-time backfill fills them across your history from your tariff (API accounts); the rare uncovered slot shows an ≈ approximation.
