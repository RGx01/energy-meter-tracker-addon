🔍 Review Unsettled Blocks

These half-hour blocks have no DCC settlement yet, so they're billed using your local CAD readings as a fallback. Recent blocks normally settle within a day or two; older ones may indicate a genuine gap.

🕳️ Missing Data

If the add-on was offline for longer than 12 hours, those half-hour blocks were never recorded and the routine settlement poll will not go back for them. Your supplier still holds the settled readings, so they can be recovered.

Recovered blocks use the authoritative half-hourly figures and are priced at the rates in force at the time, but they carry no per-device breakdown — EMT was not running, so no device readings exist for that period.

Blocks you deleted on purpose will also be restored. This is why recovery is a manual action: EMT cannot tell a deliberate deletion from an outage.
